#+title: ADR-0021: Borrowed Consumer TD Admission
#+date: 2026-10-06
#+status: Accepted

* Context

The normalized Consumer owner selected after the opaque-container accounting
counterexample required complete node/edge/byte copying, exact resealing and
post-copy equivalence. The build-only lifetime correction had already removed
TD retention from Published. Owning a second complete TD therefore adds
construction and cleanup without supplying a runtime responsibility.

Workspace 0074 challenged that choice. Workspace 0075 and the discriminator
merged in PR #128 construct the replacement for an explicit input/compiler
envelope. Independent review accepted exact head
=4b2fd2f4a77d83ab78c360202f7cd000d8fd7e17= after fixing unpaid Ready URI
validation and scope-size replay. That evidence does not establish production
global accounting, variable output cleanup, generic compiler contracts, real
publication races or bounded JSON ingestion.

* Decision

The first Consumer Property Read admission borrows a caller-owned immutable
typed =Thing=. TD performs bounded inspection of the entire supplied value and
complete shared Basic validation before minting an opaque lifetime-bearing
proof. TD owns effective operations, security and URI meaning. Planning consumes
only TD's paid semantic lending cursor: one coordinate remains Ready until
acknowledged, and every poll obtains a fresh short loan. Suspended state owns
scalars, external immutable references or owned storage, never references into
its own scratch. No complete derived TD table is required.

Planning owns complete preflight, required runtime facts, every property lookup
row, all compiler bounds before any start, sequential eager compilation, exact
output accounting and a source-independent Frozen draft. Concrete artifacts
and suspended compiler state must satisfy the owned-input/cleanup contract;
a lifetime-free wrapper alone is insufficient.

Servient owns immutable policy and complete-registration capture, paired
local/parent allowances, the admission transaction, runtime reservation,
cleanup, identity/generation checks, private publication permit and atomic
installation. All input/scratch loans end and build cleanup finishes before
the final checks and permit. Permit issuance closes cancellation under exclusive
installation authority; installation is preallocated, non-fallible,
allocation-free, callback-free and non-yielding. Published owns plans, the
separate complete registration and runtime lifecycle/resource records.

Caller storage stays live until the consumed build owner returns a source-free
result or terminal cleanup. It is a baseline, not an engine-owned allocation
estimate. Engine-owned upstream sources retain their original global charges
while lent. Controlled frame, current derived URI, output, compiler, erasure,
slot, diagnostic and cleanup capacity is charged before work/allocation; peaks
observe simultaneous owners and largest requests observe actual Layouts.

The exact API/progress, typed-content byte oracle, operation-local resource
projections, support algorithms, allocation formulas and readmission obligations
are owned by
[[file:../work-packages/WP-100-consumer-validated-thing-admission.md][the Consumer TD admission contract]],
with Planning and Servient details in their registered specifications and work
packages. This is a refinement within the existing 65-requirement v5.1 set.

Strict bounded JSON ingestion is a separate future TD capability. ADR-0020's
literal JSON algebra, shared field policies, duplicate/null/Number rules,
ordinary-serde differences and shared resumable RFC3339 decoder remain its
authority. Its complete construction, physical source accounting and terminal
proof move with that capability; they are not typed Consumer prerequisites.
Ordinary parse-and-borrow never establishes a bounded ingestion claim. Directory,
Discovery or later release claims needing external ingestion require their own
domain/capability admission and that evidence.

* Supersession and preserved guarantees

This decision supersedes the mandatory complete normalized Consumer Snapshot,
three-retained/four-temporary site counts, exact-length TD seal, opaque-map
canonical sorting, whole-TD post-copy equivalence, Snapshot footprint handoff,
owning normalized-source continuations and post-permit source destruction.
The old source-to-persistent-document reclassification removal remains required
through its owning future source change. Actual source accounts remain valid
for other owners. Legacy by-value =consume(Thing)= cannot be the bounded facade
unless a separate owning adapter proves bounded source destruction.

Complete Basic and structural rejection, the Number amendment and AP access,
finite resource/work/lifetime bounds, first-cause diagnostics, bounded cleanup,
complete Planning output, the all-bounds barrier, source/artifact independence,
generation/registration integrity, atomic publication, leases/drain and bounded
reclamation remain required. URI resolution is byte-resumable in production;
the discriminator's conservative quadratic atomic envelope is historical local
feasibility evidence, not authority to under-support a promised profile.

ADR-0008's attribution of W3C defaults/security/target resolution to Planning
is superseded by TD semantic ownership. ADR-0019's Consumer domain entry and
ADR-0015's immutable profiles/linear budgets remain. ADR-0020's coupling to a
mandatory first-Consumer Snapshot and decoder is superseded, its wire semantics
remain. Existing workspace documents, fixtures and reviewed evidence retain
their narrower historical claims; they are not rewritten as replacement proof.

No tranche/admission/gate status, requirement activation, production completion
or release claim changes. Replacement authority needs independent exact-head
acceptance; readmission remains a separate reviewed transition after the
replacement evidence obligations are discharged.

* Alternatives

Complete capacity-charged TD ownership remains appropriate for a demonstrated
owned ingestion use, but is unnecessary for typed input. A full derived table
adds another retained representation; one Ready coordinate is sufficient.
Unrestricted synchronous queries, raw Thing Planning input, caller-minted proof,
hidden source retention and an unbounded parse/drop convenience would each
evade an active semantic, progress or ownership boundary and are rejected.
