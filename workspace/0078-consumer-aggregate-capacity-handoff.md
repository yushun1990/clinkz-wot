# Consumer aggregate capacity handoff

Status: MIGRATED

## Question and reconstruction

Can the accepted paid TD continuation, complete checked compiler owner, child
ledger and lifetime-free cleanup destination compose at aggregate admission?
The TD and compiler completion claims are narrower than that question. Their
production implementations, not their candidate models, are the basis here.

ADR-0021 and the TD admission owner require borrowed complete typed meaning,
short Ready loans and a non-refilling second semantic pass. Planning requires
all owned coordinates and all bounds before start; Core's completed closed
primitive supplies callback costs and separate native/Host storage. Foundation
separates child accounting from parent authorization. Servient owns the
transaction/policy/registration association and parent/global pairing.

Review 07 and topics 0076/0077 challenge universal physical representation and
generic compiler eligibility. They do not justify replacing this shared semantic
program or the complete all-bounds failure promise. The public Host primitive is
now supported; neither the old open compiler nor the fixed TD/mock witness can
stand in for variable aggregate cleanup.

## Concrete conflict

Planning previously required `admit_runtime(AdmissionLedger)` to check the
ledger's transaction/account/policy association and return a mismatched ledger
unchanged before work. `foundation/src/resource.rs` has a public ledger
constructor, private owner/account fields and no nonce/policy identity. Equal
limits cannot certify provenance. Reserving zero bytes to discover identity
would add a synthetic accounting action, still prove no parent allowance, and
cannot supply missing policy/nonce authority.

The cleanup-slot declaration also consumed `ResourceReservation<'a>` without
explaining how its account loan ended or who retained the charge. Its private
target borrows `ResourceAccount` or `AdmissionLedger`; keeping that guard in a
source-free slot would require a lifetime and prevent ordinary root ownership.
Committing it already ends the loan and retains the charge in the original
account. Treating commit as release, or dropping that account at handoff, would
be false accounting.

## Selected boundary and alternatives

Keep parent/provenance validation at Servient's existing coordinator. Planning's
unique waiting owner keeps its preflight tied to the original proof/config/
registration; its received child ledger enforces local actual charges under
copied policy. It does not authenticate a parent or accept a detached preflight
as authority. An undersized child fails the unpublished build; publication
cannot arise from this advanced entry. Future Servient evidence must falsify
wrong-transaction/wrong-policy/reused capacity at that coordinator.

The cleanup constructor checks kind and actual inline capacity, captures fixed
charge metadata, commits on success and returns the unchanged guard on failure.
The coordinator retains the original account/parent until the destination and
all deposited/taken children are terminal. Rust provides inline alignment; the
logical capacity sum is not an allocator request. No Foundation getter, new
certificate, allocator abstraction or resource row is required.

Adding an owner getter would expose a tuple without proving provenance. A new
public attestation token would need another trusted issuer while Servient already
owns that authority. Keeping a borrowed guard in the slot introduces the exact
root/loan coupling the consumed source-free contract excludes. Moving global
authority into Planning broadens its role. These are larger or incomplete
corrections. Static-only or streaming compilation would change unrelated
accepted semantics; generated-image/runtime proposals retain topic 0076's later
evaluation boundary.

## Evidence, migration and impact

The [external discriminator](../tools/architecture-fixtures/consumer-aggregate-admission/README.md)
joins production TD to checked complete Core static/public Host registrations,
exercises two paid passes, real bounds before starts, a later bound failure,
concrete output after actual source/registration destruction, and fixed
consuming/abandoned input-owner transfers. Its committed-slot test can use the
original account after constructor return while the charge remains live;
undersized rejection returns the guard and restores live use without erasing
reservation history. Compile negatives reject raw input/compiler and premature
loan termination. This is construction evidence, not variable rollback/global
accounting or target completion.

Migration is confined to Planning's aggregate contract/constructor rejection,
Foundation's existing accounting explanation, Servient's coordinator contract,
the existing Consumer state entry
projection and the exact WP-200 admission candidate/index. API ownership already
registers these public names. No accepted TD/Core behavior, completed evidence,
Producer lifecycle, requirement identity, resource value or milestone changes.
TD/Core completion evidence is explicitly reaffirmed only at its original scope;
the fixed composition discriminator adds evidence without broadening it.
The future aggregate projection is corrected before admission, so no admitted
aggregate implementation/evidence is invalidated. No new ADR is needed: the
decision applies existing cross-domain authority to an unimplemented handoff.

Migration is not independent acceptance. The candidate still requires exact-head
review and a separate admission transition. Variable aggregate resource/work/
rollback and later Servient transaction evidence retain their own boundaries.
